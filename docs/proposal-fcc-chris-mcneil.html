Overview
What BoardVotes Does

BoardVotes organizes, searches, and analyzes Fairhope City Council meeting activity using official records, agendas, minutes, press packets, and meeting video — updated automatically on each meeting cycle with no manual effort required.

The platform gives residents, journalists, and civic leaders a single place to track every vote, every ordinance, and every council member's record — sourced directly from official city documents.

Automation
How Updates Work

All scanning and ingestion is fully automated. No manual steps are required after deployment.

Source Cadence What's Captured Tier
Council agendas Bi-weekly Agenda PDF, meeting date, items list Included
Press packets Bi-weekly Supporting documents per meeting Included
Council minutes Bi-weekly Minutes PDF, vote extraction, ordinance index Included
Work sessions & special meetings Weekly Agendas and minutes Weekly Add-On
YouTube channel Weekly New video detection, title, date, URL Weekly Add-On
Video captions Weekly Auto-caption text, keyword index Weekly Add-On
PDF & audio archive Each scan run Raw files, transcripts, JSON exports Data Add-On

Agendas are captured within 1–3 days of posting (source posts Thursday/Friday before each Monday meeting). Minutes are captured within one scan cycle of publication — source publishes within 35 days of each meeting.
